    Where this fits

    Freelance marketplaces: checks written before payment, a computed verdict, and a bond that makes recycled work cost the seller money.

    Agents buying from agents: a contract the buyer can verify in code, settled without a human on a support call.

    Small businesses: bond backed protection for digital services, without a procurement department.
